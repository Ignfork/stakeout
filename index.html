THE GUIDED TOUR

How a StakeOut works

Four stops on the tour.

01

Commission

Launch a token with its own staking pool in one go: name, ticker, image, socials, an initial buy and a target APR.

02

Stake

Holders stake straight from their wallet. No deposit, no fee: tokens stay in your wallet while they earn.

03

Refill

Creator rewards buy the token back and top up the pool, so perpetual pools keep paying with no end date.

04

Claim

Claim whenever you like. You sign and pay the tiny Solana network fee for your own payout.

Perpetual pools

No end date. Creator-reward buybacks keep refilling the reward supply for as long as the token trades.

Fixed pools

A set reward supply. The pool closes once 100% of it has been paid out to stakers.
